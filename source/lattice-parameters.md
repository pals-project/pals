## Lattice parameters

Lattice parameters are parameters that are not associated with any lattice element but with the
lattice as a whole. This section standardizes the names and meanings of such.


- `chromaticity_a`, `chromaticity_b`


- `emittance_a`, `emittance_b`, `emittance_c`


- `fractional_tune_a`, `fractional_tune_b`, `fractional_tune_c`


- `normalized_emittance_a`, `normalized_emittance_b`, `normalized_emittance_c`


- `momentum_compaction`


- `spin_tune`


- `tune_a`, `tune_b`, `tune_c`
